## Portfolio Strategy

### The Principle
> **Lead with relevance. Anchor with credibility.**

Old work proves you're legitimate. New work proves you're relevant to the buyer in front of you.

### New Work — 3 Concept Case Studies (Build These First)

These are self-initiated / speculative builds. Label them clearly as concept projects. Deploy them live — the working product is the proof, not metrics.

**1. Restaurant**
- Problem: Most Mangalore restaurants lose bookings after hours
- Build: Website + menu + WhatsApp-based table booking automation
- Outcome framing: "Bookings happen 24/7 without the owner being available"

**2. Salon / Wellness Clinic**
- Problem: Salons lose 30%+ of bookings because owners can't answer during appointments
- Build: Website + automated appointment booking + Google Business setup
- Outcome framing: "Owner saves 2 hours/day, never misses a booking"

**3. Local D2C / Retail Brand**
- Problem: Good local products with zero online presence or poor Shopify setup
- Build: Shopify store + brand refresh + basic email automation
- Outcome framing: "Brand now sells beyond Mangalore, first online orders within a week"

### How to Write Case Studies
Write them as **stories, not design showcases.**
- Lead with the human problem
- Show the live working product
- Use projected impact framed as estimates (not fake claims)
- Never fabricate numbers — use reasoned projections based on real experience

## Client Acquisition Strategy

### Primary Method — Walk-In POC Pitch (Not Cold Calls)
Mangalore is small. Physical presence beats cold calls for local SMBs.

**Process:**
1. Make a list of 30–50 businesses you'd genuinely like to work with
2. For the top 5–10, build a speculative POC first (takes 4–6 hours with AI leverage)
3. Walk in, open your laptop: *"I built this for your business. It's live. If you like it, it's yours for ₹30K and ₹8K/month maintenance."*
4. If they say no — it's a case study. If they say yes — it's your first client.

**Why this works:** The live product removes all sales friction. The owner sees exactly what they're getting before spending a rupee.

### Secondary Method — Warm Referrals
- First 2–3 clients will generate referrals naturally
- Small business owners in Mangalore know each other
- One happy restaurant client → 3 more within a month

### On Fake Numbers — Never
- Never fabricate metrics on spec work
- Use projected estimates clearly framed as such
- The live working product is more persuasive than any percentage
- Real numbers from past work (Rapido 100→1M rides, Wayground 60% Indonesia penetration) are available for Audience B
